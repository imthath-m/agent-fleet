Triage · Support Available · onboarding now

Answers routine support tickets with cited answers from your own help docs — and hands the hard ones to you.

classify→ answer + cite→ safe actions→ escalate to you
  • First-response time is embarrassing. Triage drafts a grounded answer the moment a ticket lands — from email, chat, or webform.
  • Same five questions, forever. Routine tickets get resolved automatically, with citations to your help center, not hallucinations.
  • Scared of AI going rogue? Refunds and account changes always wait for a human unless you say otherwise. Every step is logged and auditable.

Payable · Finance Available · onboarding now

Reads every invoice, checks the math, catches duplicates — and only bothers a human with the genuine exceptions.

extract→ validate math + PO→ catch duplicates→ approve or escalate
  • Invoice pile, no AP clerk. Clean invoices go straight through to your books; you approve only the flagged ones.
  • Double payments happen. Duplicate detection fingerprints every invoice, so paying the same bill twice stops being a risk.
  • Auditors ask "why was this paid?" Every invoice keeps a full audit trail: what was extracted, what was checked, who approved it.
Prospect · Sales Available · onboarding now

Researches every lead on the open web, scores it against your ideal customer, and drafts outreach you approve before anything sends.

research→ score vs your ICP→ draft w/ evidence→ you approve
  • An hour of googling per lead. Prospect builds the dossier in minutes — industry, size, tech, buying signals — every fact cited to its source.
  • Templates that scream template. Drafts are personalized from real evidence about the company, not {first_name} tricks.
  • Nothing sends without you. Every single draft waits for your approval. Your reputation stays yours.
Follow-Through · Ops Available · onboarding now

Turns every meeting into owned, dated action items — and remembers what was promised last week.

transcript in→ extract actions→ check past promises→ route + digest
  • Decisions evaporate by Friday. Every action item gets an owner, a due date, and a ticket in your tracker — automatically.
  • "Didn't you say you'd ship that?" Cross-meeting memory resurfaces last week's unresolved commitments so nothing silently dies.
  • No ops person to chase people. A digest goes to participants after every meeting; external emails always get a human OK first.
Redline · Legal Available · onboarding now

First-pass contract review against your own playbook — every flag cites the exact clause and the rule it breaks.

segment clauses→ match your playbook→ flag risks + cite→ human review
  • Every NDA is a bottleneck. Redline does the first pass in minutes and ranks findings by severity, so counsel time goes where it matters.
  • AI that cites, or it doesn't count. A finding without a verifiable citation to the clause and your rule is dropped — zero tolerance.
  • Assist, not a lawyer. A qualified human reviews every report before it's used. Nothing is sent or signed by the agent.

Redline is a review-assist tool, not legal advice.

Questions, answered straight

Can I adopt just one agent?▾

Yes. Pricing is per agent — start with the one job that hurts most. Take any combination, or all five for a bundle discount. Add more as your operations grow.

What am I committing to by getting started?▾

A conversation. You give us your email and which agent(s) you want; we reach out to scope wiring it into your stack. No card is charged from this page.

How does onboarding work?▾

We connect the agent to the systems it needs — your helpdesk, ledger, CRM, tracker, or contract folder — configure it to your policies, and go live on the agents you chose. Onboarding is per agent.

What happens to my email and answers?▾

They're stored in our form provider, used only to contact you about onboarding, never sold or shared. Reply "remove" to any email and we delete you.

What about my company's data once an agent is running?▾

Per-customer isolation, on Google Cloud. High-stakes actions (refunds, outbound email, contract findings) always require your approval. Full audit logs are a feature, not an add-on.

Is Redline giving legal advice?▾

No. Redline is a paralegal-style assist: it does a cited first pass against your own playbook, and a qualified human reviews every report. It is not a lawyer and not legal advice.
